# Code of Conduct

Version 1.0.0, effective 23 November 2026.

## 1. Scope

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
space where someone represents the project.

## 2. Expected behaviour

- Discuss the code, the evidence, and the user impact. Attack the claim, not the
  person.
- Assume a reporter has a machine in front of them that you cannot see. Ask for
  the version, the Windows build, and the log before doubting the report.
- Give a newcomer the document that answers their question, not a verdict about
  their skill.
- Respect a boundary the first time it is stated.
- Credit work that you build on.

## 3. Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language about a person's identity.
- Publishing another person's private information, including a log with a user
  name, without consent.
- Sexual attention or imagery in a project space.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem in good faith.

## 4. Reporting

Write to conduct@vellium.dev. Include what happened, where, and who was present.
Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. A maintainer who is
the subject of a report does not participate in the decision.

## 5. Enforcement

| Step | When | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Correction | A first, limited incident | A private explanation of the impact and the expected change |
| Warning | A repeated or more serious incident | A written warning with a defined boundary and a period |
| Temporary ban | A serious incident, or a warning ignored | A ban from the project spaces for a stated period |
| Permanent ban | Violence, threats, or a pattern of abuse | Permanent removal |

The maintainers document the reason and the step. An appeal goes to
conduct@vellium.dev and is read by a maintainer who was not involved.

## 6. Attribution

This code is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, and adapted to a
small project with a named reporting address.
