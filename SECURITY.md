# Security Policy

The full policy is maintained at [public/legal/security.md](public/legal/security.md).

Report a vulnerability to security@vellium.dev. Do not open a public issue.
Include the version and build commit from Settings, About, the Windows build, the
impact, and the steps that reproduce it.

We acknowledge within two business days, assess within five, and aim to fix a
high severity issue within 30 days.
